# Reward Signal: Privacy Policy

*Effective date: [publication date]* · Applies to the Reward Signal apps for iOS and Android.

**Short version: Reward Signal does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Everything stays on your device.**

## What the app does with data

- **Your code and progress** (XP, completed exercises, quiz answers, settings, the code you write, and files your code creates such as trained model weights) are stored **only on your device**, inside the app's private storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
- **Your code runs on your device.** The app includes a Python interpreter. Programs you run execute locally and have no access to the internet, your contacts, photos, location or other apps.
- **No network access.** The app works fully offline and makes no network requests. There are no analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting or third-party SDKs that collect data.
- **No account.** You never sign in or give us your name, email address or any other identifier.

## Exporting and sharing

When you choose **Export** (a code file) or **Settings → Data → Export progress**, the app hands the file to your device's system share sheet or file picker. Where it goes next is entirely your choice and is governed by the app you pick (for example Files, Drive or Mail).

## Backups

Your operating system may include the app's local storage in your own device backups (iCloud or Google backup), according to your device settings. We have no access to these backups.

## Deleting your data

Use **Settings → Data → Reset progress / Delete all saved code**, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes everything the app stored on the device.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature), the new policy will be published here with a new effective date before the change takes effect, and the app's store privacy labels will be updated.
